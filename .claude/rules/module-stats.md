### Stats

Cookieless statistics: the beacon, the day-file spool, the ingest and rollup, and the read models that the back office, `/konto` and the media kit show. No Google Analytics, no third party, no consent banner.

**Built in #47 (`venue-stats`).** Migration `Version20261103181107`; mapping `orm.mappings.Stats`; no Composer or npm package. Design change S17 (`docs/rpi-cycles.md`). Stats depends on `Venues` and `Inquiries` only; `Cms` and `Portal` may depend on Stats, nothing else may (`ModuleBoundariesTest`). Favorites knows nothing of Stats: `merkliste.js` dispatches a DOM event.

- **Flow:** `stats.js` sends `POST /_stats` → `CollectController` appends lines to `var/stats/spool/<Y-m-d>.ndjson` (Zurich day) and never opens a database connection. At 03:15 (`bin/cron-daily` step `stats-ingest`) or on the staff button, `app:stats:ingest` loads the files into raw `stats_events` and rebuilds `stats_daily`. Every screen reads `stats_daily` only.
- **Deliberate deviation from `cqrs.md`:** `CollectController` calls `Stats\Spool` directly and dispatches no command. `command.bus` wraps each handler in `doctrine_transaction`, and `beginTransaction()` connects to Postgres, which would wake Neon on every page view. `CollectControllerTest::test_collecting_never_connects_to_the_database` pins it: no firewall matches `/_stats`, the limiter's lock is `flock` and its pool is `cache.stats_collect` on `cache.app` (`var/share/<env>/pools/app`), and no Shared, Admin or Portal listener queries on that path. The ingest goes through the bus as usual.
- **Beacon request (C1):** `_stats_collect` `POST /_stats`, unlocalized, a `URLSearchParams` body (no preflight). Params: `p` page type (`PageType`, unknown → `other`), `v` the detail page's venue number (`^[1-9]\d{0,9}$`), `u` the path (cut to 255), `r` the referrer (host only, dropped when it is ours), `w=1` under `navigator.webdriver`, `e[]` events. Events, one regex each: `view`, `imp:<n>.<n>…` (up to 100 numbers, deduplicated, 0 and out-of-int4 numbers dropped), `click:<kind>` (`ClickKind`: `website|email|phone|map|instagram`; needs `v`), `add:<n>`. `BeaconPayload::fromRequest()` (static, `MAX_EVENTS` = 50) drops a malformed event and keeps the rest; texts are `mb_scrub`bed, NUL-stripped and cut to 255; the referrer host is lower-cased. Answers: 403 when `Sec-Fetch-Site` is present and not `same-origin`, or absent and `Origin` is missing or not ours; 204 with nothing written for a body over `CollectController::MAX_BODY_BYTES` (8192, `Content-Length`, else the body read), a rate-limited client (`stats_collect`: 120 per 10 minutes per client IP, sliding window) or no valid event; else 204. Always `private, no-store`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, no session, no cookie. `/_stats` is in `RobotsController::DISALLOW`.
- **Spool line (C2):** `ValueObject\SpoolEvent`, one NDJSON object per event: `at`, `type` (`EventType`: `page_view|impression|click|merkliste_add|inquiry`), `kind`, `venues` (null for a page view off the detail page, else 1–100 ints), `page`, `path`, `ref`, `ua`, `vh`, `bot`. Its constructor checks what the columns enforce (click ⇔ kind, venue numbers in 1..2147483647, texts valid UTF-8 without NUL and ≤ 255, `vh` 32 hex) and throws `\InvalidArgumentException`; `fromArray()` throws the same on a wrong shape, so a line that parses always inserts. `SpoolEvent::inquiry($at, $number)` builds the inquiry line (`page`, `path`, `ref`, `ua`, `vh` null, `bot` false); `day()` is its Zurich day; `toJson()`. The IP is never written anywhere.
- **Visitor hash and salt:** `VisitorHash::of($request, $day)` = `substr(hash_hmac('sha256', ip."\n".ua, salt), 0, 32)`. The salt is 32 random bytes in `var/stats/salt/<Y-m-d>`, created with `fopen($path, 'x')`; a second writer that loses the race reads it (up to 20 tries of 5 ms while it is empty). The prune deletes every salt of a day before today, after which no hash links to an IP again. That is what `/datenschutz`'s "Statistik" section promises.
- **Rotation (C3):** `Spool::append(list<SpoolEvent>)` groups by each event's Zurich day, one write per file: `mkdir -p`, `fopen('a')`, `flock(LOCK_EX)`, then compares the open inode with the path's and reopens (3 tries, then `\RuntimeException`) if an ingest renamed it in between, then one `fwrite`. `SpoolReader`: `claimAll()` renames every `<Y-m-d>.ndjson`, today's open one included, to `<Y-m-d>.<8 hex>.claimed`; `claimed()` lists every claimed name (sorted, so by day); `unloaded($logged)` drops the names `stats_ingest_files` already holds; `read($file)` locks the file first (a writer that locked before the rename finishes), yields `SpoolEvent`s and returns the count of skipped lines through `getReturn()` (an empty line is not counted); `delete($file)` accepts a file already gone. File names are matched against the day and claimed patterns before any path is built. `StatsPaths::spoolDir()` / `saltDir()` read `stats.dir` (`var/stats`; in test `%kernel.cache_dir%/stats`, so every run starts empty).
- **Ingest:** `app:stats:ingest` (`Console\IngestStatsCommand`, prints `<files> files, <events> events loaded, <skipped> lines skipped, <days> days rebuilt.`) dispatches `Command\IngestStats`; `IngestStatsHandler` returns `ValueObject\IngestResult` (`files`, `events`, `skipped`, `days`). Inside the bus transaction: `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('stats_ingest'))`, so the button and the cron never interleave; claim; load each unlogged claimed file in multi-row DBAL inserts of 500 (no entity hydration) and log its name in `stats_ingest_files` with `created_at` from the clock; `StatsRollup::rebuild($days)` for the days the loaded events touch; then `PruneStatsSpool` with `DispatchAfterCurrentBusStamp`, on every run, also when nothing loaded. `PruneStatsSpoolHandler` deletes only claimed files whose names are logged, plus past salts. A crash leaves its `.claimed` file unlogged, and the next run loads it; a rerun never loads a line twice.
- **Rollup (C5):** `StatsRollup::rebuild(list<Y-m-d>)` deletes a day's rows and computes them again (never adds), one `INSERT … SELECT` per family and day over `[00:00 Zurich, next 00:00 Zurich)`. `Metric` values: site rows (`venue_number = 0`) `page_view` (visitors = the day's distinct hashes, "Besuche") and `page_view_<page>` per `PageType`; venue rows `detail_view` (a page view on the detail page), `impression` (one per card number), `click_<kind>`, `merkliste_add`, `inquiry`. Venue rows count only numbers that exist in `venues.number`, so a forged number makes no row. Visitors are distinct hashes per metric, so 0 for `inquiry`; screens show events only there. A bad day string throws `\InvalidArgumentException`.
- **`automated`:** the beacon sends `w=1` under `navigator.webdriver` instead of staying silent, so raw rows keep the signal and Lighthouse still exercises the beacon. The rollup leaves those rows out everywhere. There is no other bot filter yet; because raw rows are kept forever and days are rebuilt whole, a later filter can recompute history.
- **Tables (C4):** `stats_events` (raw, append-only, kept forever; `venues` JSONB, `idx_stats_events_occurred_at`), `stats_daily` (PK `day, venue_number, metric`, `idx_stats_daily_venue_number_day`, rows replaced per day), `stats_ingest_files` (`uk_stats_ingest_files_file_name`, append-only). Entities `Entity\StatsEvent`, `DailyStat`, `IngestedSpoolFile` exist for the mapping and `schema:validate` only; the handler and the rollup write with DBAL. CHECK names: `chk_stats_events_type`, `_kind`, `_page`, `_click_kind`, `chk_stats_daily_metric`, and the non-negative ones.
- **Inquiries:** `EventListener\RecordInquiryListener` listens to `Inquiries\Event\InquirySubmitted` (after the commits), finds the venue with `ReadModel\InquiryVenueQuery::numberOf($inquiryId)` and appends one `inquiry` line; nothing for an inquiry without a venue. It catches `\Throwable` itself and logs class, file and line, never the message, because a throwing subscriber would stop the CRM's listener of the same event.
- **Read models** (every `from`/`to`/`today` is a `\DateTimeImmutable` of which only the date counts, both ends inclusive; empty days and months are zero-filled; returned dates are midnight Europe/Zurich):
  - `SiteStatsQuery`: `summary($from, $to): SiteSummary` (`visits`, `pageViews`, `detailViews`, `contactClicks`, `merklisteAdds`, `inquiries`; the venue figures come from venue rows, so forged numbers stay out); `dailyPageViews()` and `dailyVisits()`: `list<DailyPoint>` (`day`, `value`); `pageTypes(): list<PageTypeRow>` (`pageType`, `pageViews`, `visitors`; all six, most views first); `venues(): list<VenueRow>` (`number`, `name`, `detailViews`, `impressions`, `clicks` over all kinds, `merklisteAdds`, `inquiries`; venues with a row in range, most detail views first); `months($today, positive-int $count = 12): list<MonthRow>` (`month` plus `SiteSummary`'s six, oldest first, today's month included); `lastIngestAt(): ?\DateTimeImmutable` (the newest `stats_ingest_files.created_at`).
  - `VenueStatsQuery`: `summary($number, $from, $to): VenueSummary` (`detailViews`, `detailVisitors`, `impressions`, `clicks` as `array<string,int>` keyed by `ClickKind` value with all five keys, `clickTotal`, `merklisteAdds`, `inquiries`); `dailyDetailViews($number, $from, $to): list<DailyPoint>`.
  - `ValueObject\StatsPeriod` (7, 30, 90, 365 days, today included): `fromQuery(?string)` reads `?zeitraum=` (unknown → 30), `firstDay($today)` (not `from()`: a backed enum has the static one). Callers read `zeitraum` from `query->all()`, so `zeitraum[]=` falls back instead of a 400.
- **Figures read "Stand: <last ingest>",** not "bis gestern", because the staff button can load today's events.
- **Browser (`assets/js/stats.js`):** loaded on every public page by one line in `templates/base.html.twig` after `merkliste.js`: `<script type="module" … data-stats-url data-stats-page="{% block stats_page %}…" data-stats-venue="{% block stats_venue %}">`. The page blocks set `home`, `listing`/`landing` (listing), `detail` plus the venue number (detail), `merkliste`; base's default is `other`, written `{% set page_type = 'other' %}{{ page_type }}` so `TemplatesHaveNoRawTextTest` passes. It does nothing under `navigator.globalPrivacyControl` or without `sendBeacon`; no cookie, no storage. It queues `view` at once and sends after `load`; an `IntersectionObserver` (threshold 0.5) on `[data-stats-venue]:not(script)` (the `<article class="card">` of `search/_card`; `:not(script)` skips its own tag) collects card numbers once each; a capture-phase click listener on `[data-stats-click]` (`venues/detail/_contact` website, email, phone, instagram; `_map` load button and "open map" link) queues a click and sends at once, before `phone-reveal.js` swaps the link; `merkliste:add` (from `merkliste.js`) queues an add. It flushes on `visibilitychange` to hidden and on `pagehide`, at most 50 events per beacon (more beacons if needed) and 100 numbers per `imp`. It sends `w=1` under `navigator.webdriver`.
- **Templates and strings:** `templates/admin/stats/_venue_figures.html.twig` (under `admin/` because it includes UI kit components, which `PublicPagesLoadNoAdminAssetsTest` keeps out of public folders), shared by the CMS tab and the `/konto` tab, included `with_context = false` with `summary` (`VenueSummary`), `daily` (`list<DailyPoint>`), `period` (`StatsPeriod`), `period_links` (`list<{days, href, active}>`: each caller maps `StatsPeriod::cases()` to its own route), `as_of` (`?\DateTimeImmutable`), `own_traffic_note` (bool, true on `/konto`). It renders its own "Stand" line (`.stats-as-of`), the period tabs, the KPI row and `_bar_chart`. Its strings are in the new domain `stats` (`translations/stats+intl-icu.de.yaml`, `venue.*`), because Portal templates cannot use `cms` strings. The staff screens' strings are in `cms` under `stats.*`.
- **Screens (in other modules):** `/admin/statistik` (`admin_stats`, button `admin_stats_refresh`), `/admin/statistik/mediakit` (`admin_stats_media_kit`, CSV `admin_stats_media_kit_csv`), the venue tab `admin_locations_stats` and the dashboard widget are in `### Cms`; the manager's tab `portal_location_stats` is in `### Portal`.
- **Tests:** `tests/Unit/Stats/{BeaconPayloadTest, SpoolTest, SpoolReaderTest, VisitorHashTest, StatsPeriodTest}`, `tests/Integration/Stats/{IngestStatsHandlerTest, IngestStatsCommandTest, RecordInquiryListenerTest, SiteStatsQueryTest, VenueStatsQueryTest, StatsVocabularyChecksTest}` (the enums match the CHECKs), `tests/Application/Stats/{CollectControllerTest, BeaconMarkupTest}`, `admin-ui/tests/public/stats.test.js` (Vitest + jsdom). `BeaconMarkupTest` asserts the "open map" link's `map` kind only: the test env has no Maps key, so "Karte laden" is not rendered there.

**G8 (`funnel-scorecard`): the funnel log and scorecard.** The event-level log behind the staff screen `/admin/statistik/funnel` (`### Cms`), because `stats_daily` rows are aggregates and cannot give cohorts or weeks. Migration `Version20261107150513`, table `funnel_events` (`id`, `occurred_at`, `event`, `venue_id`, `campaign_code`), mapping-only `Entity\RecordedFunnelEvent`. No personal data by construction.

- **Write: `Shared\Command\RecordFunnelEvent(FunnelEvent $event, ?string $venueId = null, ?string $campaignCode = null)`** is the only way to add a row; `Shared\Funnel\FunnelEvent` is the closed list in funnel order (`preview_opened`, `claim_started`, `claim_verified`, `venue_published`, `inquiry_delivered`, `pro_paid`, `subscription_cancelled`, `brief_sent`). Every module may dispatch it (Shared; Crm may not use Stats). **Inside a command handler dispatch it with `[new DispatchAfterCurrentBusStamp()]`**: the row is then written after your commit and not at all when your command fails (`DispatchAfterCurrentBusMiddleware` drops the queue); from a controller or console command dispatch it plainly. `CommandHandler\RecordFunnelEventHandler` is best effort and **never throws**: it drops (stores as null, and logs the field name only) a venue id that is no UUID or a campaign code that is not a plain `Shared\Funnel\CampaignCode` (`[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9_-]{0,39}`, no `@`, no `.`), so the step itself is always recorded; it inserts with DBAL in a savepoint (`Connection::transactional()` nests with savepoints, so a failed insert cannot abort the caller's transaction, and a failed flush cannot close the request's entity manager), and logs `Funnel event {event}: not recorded ({exception_class} at {file}:{line})` when the insert fails, never a value. Only the bus's own transaction around it could still fail. The time is the clock's. Staff-entered campaign codes (G5, G11) should be checked with `CampaignCode::isValid()` where they are typed in; a code never comes from a visitor's request.
- **Table rules.** No foreign key on `venue_id` (a row outlives its venue, as `stats_events.venues`). `chk_funnel_events_event` lists the enum in order and `chk_funnel_events_campaign_code` holds the code pattern; `StatsVocabularyChecksTest` pins the first, so **a new `FunnelEvent` case needs a migration that replaces the CHECK**. Indexes: `(event, occurred_at)`, `(campaign_code)`, `(venue_id, occurred_at)` (the first-touch probe). Tests that count rows must `DELETE FROM funnel_events` first inside their rolled-back transaction; a test that commits (`SubmitInquiryConcurrencyTest`) deletes its own rows.
- **Emitters wired now:** `venue_published` (`Venues\CommandHandler\PublishVenueHandler`: every transition to online, a republish counts again) and `inquiry_delivered` (`Inquiries\CommandHandler\DeliverInquiryHandler`: once per inquiry, right after the venue mail went out). Left to their cycles: `preview_opened` (G5), `claim_started`/`claim_verified` (G6), `pro_paid`/`subscription_cancelled` (G9), `brief_sent` (F13: one brief once, `venueId` null). G6 and G7 must not emit `venue_published` / `inquiry_delivered` again. The scope text's `venue_preview_opened` and `reverse_match_completed` are `preview_opened` and `brief_sent` here.
- **Read: `ReadModel\FunnelScorecardQuery::scorecard(FunnelWindow, FunnelFilter, $now): FunnelScorecard`**, four statements (weeks, totals, per campaign, paying). Weeks are ISO weeks in Europe/Zurich, every week of the window listed (newest first, zeros included); `ValueObject\FunnelWindow` is 4/12/26/52 weeks (`?wochen=`, default 12), `ValueObject\FunnelFilter` is all / without campaign / one code (`?kampagne=`: absent all, `-` without). A venue step counts distinct venues (per week, per campaign or per window), `inquiry_delivered` and `brief_sent` count rows, a venue step without a venue counts each row. **Campaign of an event** = its own code, else the first code ever recorded for that venue if that first touch was at or before the event (a LATERAL probe), else none; so Venues and Inquiries emit without knowing a campaign, and what a venue did before any campaign reached it stays "Ohne Kampagne". `FunnelPaying` is the critical metric of CMO 9.1: venues with a `pro_paid` in the last 365 days and how many of them got an `inquiry_delivered` since their first payment; `withQualifiedInquiry` is null until G7 supplies it (the screen shows "n/a"; that field and `FunnelScorecardQuery::paying()` are the seam).
- **Query ceiling.** Each screen reads one bounded time range of `funnel_events` (at most 52 weeks) three times plus the `pro_paid` rows of 12 months, and outputs at most 52 weeks x 8 steps and one row per campaign. Measured on the dev Postgres with a realistic mix (45 % previews, 36 % inquiries, few payments) spread evenly over 52 weeks and the 52-week window (the worst case, since the default window is 12 weeks): about 0.5 s for the four statements at 30,000 rows, 0.9 s at 100,000, 3 s at 300,000, 12 s at 1,000,000 (the grouped DISTINCT sort and the per-venue probe dominate). Expected volume is tens of thousands of rows a year. **Past about 300,000 rows in the shown window, add a weekly rollup table (like `stats_daily`) instead of reading events.** `FunnelScorecardQueryTest` pins the statement count at 4.
- **Seeds and tests.** `Story\FunnelStory` (loaded by `AppStory`; skips when its two campaigns are already in the log, throws when fewer than 24 venues are published; no event later than now) writes ten weeks, two campaigns and two paying venues over the first 24 demo venues; `Factory\RecordedFunnelEventFactory`. `tests/Unit/Stats/{FunnelWindowTest, FunnelFilterTest}`, `tests/Unit/Shared/CampaignCodeTest`, `tests/Integration/Stats/{RecordFunnelEventHandlerTest, FunnelScorecardQueryTest, FunnelStoryTest}` (incl. the CHECK that refuses an address), `tests/Integration/Cms/FunnelTranslationKeysTest`, `tests/Integration/Venues/PublishVenueFunnelTest`, `tests/Integration/Portal/AcceptSubmissionFunnelTest` (the nested publish: the step follows the outer commit and goes with a rollback), `tests/Application/Inquiries/SubmitInquiryFunnelTest` (the full pipeline, and `funnel_events::text` holds no `@`), `tests/Application/Cms/FunnelControllerTest`; `ModuleBoundariesProbeTest` has clean Crm and Portal fixtures that dispatch the command. `BaselineMigrationTest` now also rolls back and replays every later migration.
