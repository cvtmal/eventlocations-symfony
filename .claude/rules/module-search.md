### Search

Holds `SearchCriteria` and its URL codec, the listing and its filters, landing pages, the home page, the near-me page and similar venues. Read it by hand before touching `search/_card` (contract under "Card partial") or `SearchCriteria`. Search never imports Seo (templates call Seo's Twig functions).

## SearchCriteria and the URL codec

- **Hotspot** (F5 owns it, `docs/rpi-cycles.md` "Running cycles in parallel"): `Search\ReadModel\SearchCriteria` owns the parameter names and the codec; filter UIs only render it, other cycles only read it. Add lines, never restructure. Build with `none()`, `fromQuery($request->query, $knownTermsQuery->all())`, `withPage()`, `withCanton()`; `KnownTermsQuery` loads `taxonomy_terms` once per request (`kernel.reset`).
- **Parameters:** `canton`; `place` (town UUID or 4-digit ZIP) + `radius` (0 to 50 km, default 5, kept only with a place, 0 = that town or ZIP only); `guests` (a plain number 1 to 50000, canonical as given without leading zeros: `?guests=080` links as `80`; F5 removed `GuestBand`); `q` (whitespace collapsed, at most 100 characters, needs a letter or digit); `event[] setting[] building[] infra[] furniture[] tech[] catering[] surroundings[] amenity[]` (`SearchCriteria::PARAMS`, in `toQuery()` order; `event` = `suitable_for`) (known codes of the matching `TermGroup`; `infra`/`tech` = `infrastructure`/`technology`); `access[]` (`Access`); `rooms` 1 to 6; `area` one of `AREAS` (50 to 300); `height` one of `HEIGHTS` (2.5 to 5.0); `page` 1 to 1000. F3 dropped `ceremony`, `usage` and `curfew`, F5 `price[]` and the wedding amenity codes (an old URL with them is dropped silently).
- **Invalid values are dropped, never a 4xx/5xx:** `fromQuery()` reads `$query->all()` once and type-checks (`get()` throws on a scalar/array mismatch); numbers have at most 5 digits.
- **`toQuery()` is canonical:** fixed key order (`page` last, `access` right after `setting`), lists sorted and deduplicated, page 1 and radius 5 omitted. Build URLs with `listing_path()` or `http_build_query($criteria->toQuery())`, never by hand: one filter state, one URL.
- **`FILTER_CODES`** lists the only codes that filter (`amenity`: `parking, public_transport, wheelchair_access, overnight`, F5); an unlisted group filters on all its codes. `SearchCriteria::offers($group, $code)` is the one check: `fromQuery()` drops `amenity[]=tent`, `FilterDataQuery::terms()` shows no control for it. A code the URL accepts needs a control, or it narrows results invisibly (`FilterDataQueryTest`).

## Read side

- **`ListingQuery`:** `results()` runs 3 queries (cards, photos, count; 2 on an empty page) and returns a `ListingPage` (`PER_PAGE` 48); `first($limit, $photosPerCard, $locale)` is the home Spotlight; `cardsByNumbers(list<int>, $locale, $photosPerCard = 8)` returns the published venues among the numbers in the given order (`/merkliste`, `nearby`). `CARD_COLUMNS`/`CARD_TOWN_JOIN` serve every card query: change the town join once.
- **Order (S6):** `tiers.rank_weight DESC` (F12), then `md5(id || seed)` (seed = today in Europe/Zurich from `ClockInterface`, `MockClock` in tests), then `id`.
- **Cards:** no rating (the Google rating is on the detail page only, `### Venues`); up to 8 venue-level `CardPhoto`s from `to_jsonb(venue_photos)`; `card_picture()` builds a `Picture` only when `hasVariants()`. A venue without a town is a card and counted (`LEFT JOIN towns`; `ListingCard::$place` is a `VenuePlace`, `### Venues`, and `_card_facts` draws the pin only when it is known; `SimilarVenuesQuery` ranks with `(...) IS TRUE`, a NULL canton sorts first under `DESC`).
- **`ListingSql`:** `new ListingSql(SearchCriteria::none(), ...)` is the one definition of "published" (`status = 'published'` plus a default-locale translation), reused by every other query. Groups AND; inside a group `event`, `setting`, `building` are OR (`ListingSql::OR_GROUPS`), every other term group and `access` AND. Guests ("fits N", F5b): the venue's own range fits (`capacity_max >= :guests AND (capacity_min IS NULL OR capacity_min <= :guests)`) OR `EXISTS` a room whose `rooms.capacity_{min,max}` (F4's layout-derived range) fits the same way; a room without a maximum fits nobody. The SQL is `Venues\VenueCapacity::guestsFitSql('v', 'guests')` since G7, which Inquiries' qualification shares. Access thresholds `Access::maxDistanceKm()` (1/10/30 km, NULL = not near) via `ListingSql::accessColumn()`.
- **Indexes** (`idx_venues_earth` GiST on `ll_to_earth(lat, lng)`, `idx_venues_search_key` GIN trigram on `geo_search_key(name)`): the radius centre must be a joined `MATERIALIZED` CTE (`WITH centre ... CROSS JOIN centre c`) or the planner ignores the GiST; an unknown town or ZIP leaves it empty. Name search folds through `geo_fold_de`/`geo_fold`, guarded by `geo_fold_de(:q) <> ''`.
- **`FilterDataQuery`** (`kernel.reset`): whether a published venue carries a filter's data (not faceted). `has($key)`: `place` (lat/lng set: the radius measures from the venue's own pin), `town`, `rooms`, `area`, `height`, `access.station|highway|airport` (a venue within `Access::maxDistanceKm()`, not merely a non-NULL distance); an unknown key throws `\InvalidArgumentException`. `terms(TermGroup, $active = [])`: codes with data or active, in vocabulary order (`primary_position NULLS LAST`, then `position`). One memoized statement each.
- **Endpoints** (unlocalized, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, no session or cookie, TTLs in `PublicCacheHeadersTest`): `_search_count` `GET /_search/count?<listing query>` answers `{"count": n}`; `_search_places` `GET /_search/places?q=` the suggestions. Both read the query through `all()` (`?q[]=` is no 400).

- **Landing pages live in Search, not Seo** (`listing_path()` builds their URLs). Threshold `%landing.min_venues%` (5, `when@dev` 3) on `ListingSql`'s published predicate, one rule for canton, town and building pages: a page switches on and off by data alone, and below it is a 404. `landing_links()` feeds "Andere suchten auch nach": the top 9 cantons then building pages, a canton page's towns first, a town page's siblings and canton first; live pages only, never the current one.
  - **`LandingPages`** (`kernel.reset`): one memoized `UNION ALL` statement gives every live `LandingPage` (`type` canton|town|building; `facetQuery()`, `consumes()`); `candidates()` is the same SQL at threshold 1 for the back office (not memoized, never writes the footer item). `keyPrefix()` is `canton.<code>`, `town.<towns.id>` or `building.<code>` (the identity, never the slug, so a renamed slug keeps its texts); `blockKey($f)` is `landing.<prefix>.<f>`.
  - **`LandingFooter`:** pool `cache.landing_pages` (no expiry), item `footer_cantons`, written by `LandingPages` only when the list changed; `cantons()` only reads (`[]` on a miss), so error pages render the footer without a query.
  - **`Search\LandingPaths::locate(toQuery())`** gives `[?LandingPage, rest]`: a town page for a live `place` with `radius=0` in its canton (or no canton), else the canton page, else exactly one live `building` code, else none; `url()`, `path()`. Every listing URL goes through it.
  - **Copy** (`search`): `landing.canton.*`, `landing.town.*`, `landing.building.<code>.{slug,heading,intro}` for all 39 building codes (headings at most 36 characters so `{heading} | {site}` fits 60). A new building term needs its three keys (`LandingCatalogueTest`). `landing.canton.heading`/`intro` take `canton_code` (no "Kanton" before Liechtenstein; callers must pass it, `### Seo`); `li` filters like any canton, follows the threshold, and has no map path, so choosing it presses none.
  - **Staff text:** `ListingIntroBlock` (`listing.intro`, 600, no fallback) shows under the h1 of the unfiltered listing, page 1 only, not when `seo.noindex`; a landing page's `intro_html` is `content_block(landing.blockKey('intro'), <catalogue sentence>)`. Staff HTML is styled by the mixin `staff-text.flow` (`assets/styles/abstracts/_staff-text.scss`; listing, home and the footer note): it wraps any word or URL, lets a code block scroll inside the column and sizes headings at 1em below the page's h1.

## Near-me page

- **`NearbyController`** (`nearby`, `GET /eventlokal-in-meiner-naehe`, `PublicCache::page()` in every state, route default `_csp: google_maps`, `### Shared`). `NearbyPoint::fromQuery()` refuses anything but plain decimal strings inside lat 45.5 to 48.1, lng 5.5 to 10.9 (CH + 30 km) and rounds to 2 dp (`NearbyPoint::town()` keeps a typed place unrounded: its centre is the listing radius's). `NearbyCentres` turns `lat`/`lng` (they win) or `ort` (a 4-digit ZIP via `PlaceResolver`, else the first town of `PlaceLookup::suggest()`) into a `NearbyRequest`; a non-string `ort` counts as absent, nothing throws, a miss is a `not_found` or `position` notice on a 200. Any of `lat`, `lng`, `ort` in the query makes the page `noindex, follow` with the bare canonical.
- `NearbyVenuesQuery::within()` (at most `RADIUS_KM` 30, `LIMIT` 100) and `all()` (`ALL_CAP` 400) reuse `ListingSql`'s published predicate. `NearbyPins` groups venues by 4-dp point into the `data-near-me-pins` JSON `[{a, o, v: [{n, u, p, k}]}]` (`k`, the distance, only with one) and owns `km()` (whole km, at least 1). Nothing within 30 km links to the listing at `SearchCriteria::MAX_RADIUS` around the typed ZIP or town (`NearbyCentre::listingPlace`), or `TownLocator::nearest()` for a position, else the plain listing.
- **Map:** only with `GOOGLE_MAPS_JS_KEY`; keyless there is no map element, no pin query, no Google string in the HTML. The box has a fixed height from first paint, so `assets/js/near-me.js` (at most 4 KB gzip) swapping its fallback line for the canvas shifts nothing. It loads the Maps script with `loading=async` and `callback=nearMeMapReady` and builds the map from that callback, never from the script's `load` event (it fires before `google.maps.importLibrary` exists); `error` and `gm_authFailure` show the fallback. Key and Map ID sit in cached HTML: a change needs a purge (`docs/operations.md`).

## Deferred card carousels (`f16-listing-lcp`, 2026-11-09)

- `search/_card` embeds `components/_carousel` with `defer: true` → `data-carousel-defer`. Under `@media (scripting: enabled)` the slides after the first hide their `<picture>` (the dominant-colour placeholder shows) until `carousel.js` sets `data-awake`: on the first `pointerenter`, `touchstart`, `focusin` or track scroll, or when the carousel is within half a viewport below the fold a second after `load`. Without scripting every picture shows.
- Why: the browser fetched the lazy images of every off-screen slide near the viewport at once (34 images, 128 KB against the 5 KB LCP image), and Lighthouse counted them: `/eventlokale` LCP went from 2,027–2,029 ms to 1,886–1,952 ms on the perf stack (demo ×1). Only the card defers (`CardCarouselDeferTest`); the detail hero, gallery, rooms and similar band do not.
- A new card surface that renders several photos must load `carousel.js`, or its later pictures stay hidden.
