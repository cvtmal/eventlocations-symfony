### Crm

Back-office companies (Locations) with their contact card, deals, tasks, activities and inquiries. Since #55 a contact is a row on its Location's card, never a record of its own, and couples are no contacts.

**Glossary (#54):** on screens the CRM company is a **Location**; the code and tables keep `Company`/`crm_companies`, routes `admin_crm_companies*`, JSON `/admin/api/crm/companies…`, the ICU placeholder `companies` and `MergeFields::$companyName` (Q11). The venue sense on CRM screens is **Eintrag**. `/konto`'s and the public site's "Location" (a venue) are unchanged; Portal's staff screens say Eintrag (OD1a).

**Built in #27 (#27a `crm-foundation`, #27b `crm-timeline`).** Migration `Version20261031102722`; mapping `orm.mappings.Crm`; no Composer or npm package; no cron. Outcome: `docs/rpi-cycles.md` `### 27.`.

- **Tables:** `crm_companies` (its `venue_id` is the Eintrag, unique, SET NULL with the venue; `crm_company_venues` was dropped by #56), `crm_contacts`, `crm_activity_types` (rows, so a new type is an insert), `crm_activities`, `crm_activity_companies`, `crm_tasks` (`crm_activity_contacts` was dropped by #55). `crm_contacts.email` is lower-case (`ContactEmail::normalise()`), NULL when empty, unique per Location since #55. `crm_tasks`: `due_on` required, `chk_crm_tasks_one_object` allows at most one of company and deal, or none (#55). `Activity::$payload` holds an empty payload as `\stdClass`, because `[]` fails `chk_crm_activities_payload`. All times are `TIMESTAMP(0)`.
- **Routes:** server pages `admin_crm_companies*` `/admin/crm/locations…` (the Eintrag's assign/release POSTs `…/{id}/eintraege…`; the contact card's POSTs `admin_crm_companies_contacts_{create,edit,remove}` `…/{id}/kontakte…`, #55), `admin_crm_contacts_export` `/admin/crm/kontakte/export`, `admin_crm_tasks` `/admin/crm/aufgaben`, `admin_crm_tasks_toggle`, `admin_crm_company_from_venue` `/admin/crm/eintraege/{venueId}/location`, `admin_crm_firmen_redirect*` (`LegacyCompanyPathController`, five GET 301s from the pre-#54 `/admin/crm/firmen…` paths, query kept, export `ROLE_ADMIN`), `admin_crm_kontakte_redirect*` (`LegacyContactPathController`, #55: `/admin/crm/kontakte` and `/admin/crm/kontakte/{uuid}` 301). JSON `admin_api_crm_company_property`, `admin_api_crm_{companies,venues}` (lookups), `admin_api_crm_activity_{log,edit,delete}`, `admin_api_crm_company_timeline`, `admin_api_crm_tasks`. Paths take UUIDs only, so the route walker covers them untouched. `ROLE_EDITOR` by class; `ROLE_ADMIN` for CSV and company delete.
- **JSON pattern (for #28 to #30):** a thin controller, class-level `#[IsGranted]` and `#[IsCsrfTokenValid('admin_api', …)]`, payload parsed by `Crm\CrmApi` into a `Crm\Form` DTO, failures through `CrmApi::failure()`. 422 `{"errors": {"field": [...]}}`, 409 and 404 on `""`. `adminFetch` drops the 403, 404 and 409 bodies, so the island needs its own labels (`admin-ui/src/crm/failure.js`: `failureOf()`, `formFailure()`). A 2xx must have a JSON body or be a 204: a 2xx non-JSON is a lost session to `adminFetch`.
- **Inline edit:** `PATCH …/{id}` with `{"field", "value", "expected"}`. `expected` is the field's loaded value, the version (S12). `Crm\ValueObject\{CompanyProperty, DealProperty}` allow-list the fields (`ContactProperty`'s five cases only feed the card dialog's rules since #55, OD-P9), `Crm\Form\PropertyConstraints` holds each field's rules for both the inline edit and the create form, `PropertyValue::canonical()` is the one canonical form (`DealProperty::canonical()` builds on it: Rappen for an amount, `Y-m-d` for a date). Island `record-properties`; `crm:property-saved` drives `[data-crm-follow]` and `[data-crm-refresh]` (`assets/js/admin/crm-record.js`).
- **No-op rule (P1, for every later command):** `AuditLogMiddleware` writes a row after every successful `AuditableCommand` and has no skip, so a handler returning early still leaves one. Read the current state and do not dispatch when nothing changes, as the property controllers, `TaskToggleController` and `CompanyRecordController::link()` do. The handler keeps its own check under the lock. Two simultaneous identical saves can leave one extra row, accepted. A summary never holds a value or a text, and is never empty (`changes` must be a JSON object).
- **Timeline:** `Crm\ReadModel\TimelineQuery::page()`, `Crm\TimelineProps::of()` (the island's whole props, first page included, one statement beyond `page()`, `EmailTemplateQuery::options()`, and only on a record with an address), `Crm\TimelineApi`. System rows come from `AuditLogQuery::forSubjects()` with `TimelineQuery::SYSTEM_COMMANDS`, on a company with no type filter only. Both sources have UUID ids and whole-second times, so one `(time, id)` pair orders the merge and the keyset. `Crm\OwnActivity::lock()` is the write side of `TimelineEntry::canBeEditedBy()`: own, composable, not anonymised. Bodies are plain text. An entry on a deal also links its company.
- **A new entry type:** a row in your migration, a `TimelineActivityType` in your `Extension` layer, a line in `admin-ui/src/timeline/types.js`, then `activity.type.<code>` and `activity.tab.<code>` for every visible type, composable or not, and `activity.compose.<code>.{placeholder,submit}` for a composable one, added to `CrmTranslationKeysTest`'s list. `TimelineTypesPinTest` holds rows, registered types and `types.js` lines equal, and checks `activity.tab.<code>`.
- **`RecordKind`:** `Company`, `Deal` (`Contact` went with #55). A new case makes PHPStan flag `recordTable()`, `linkTable()`, `linkColumn()`, `taskColumn()` and the `match` in `LogActivityHandler` and `CreateTaskHandler`. Beyond that: a link table, a `crm_tasks` column with a replaced `chk_crm_tasks_one_object`, `Task`'s constructor, a thin timeline controller, the island's `kind` prop, and `ActivityRepository::deleteUnlinked()`.
- **Tasks:** `Crm\ReadModel\TaskListQuery` (`page()`, `openOf()`, `mine()`, `overdue()`, `isDone()`), `TaskView` (`alle`, `meine`, `heute`, `ueberfaellig`, in tab order; `alle` is the default, owner 2026-11-03, so the heading's count on the bare URL includes done tasks), `crm_task_due(dueOn, done = false)` → `{label, tone}`, partials `task/{_card,_toggle,_due,_object}`. Heute and Überfällig hold everyone's tasks. "Today" is `TaskDueDate::today($clock->now())`, Europe/Zurich. A company's card and the venue panel also collect the tasks on its deals. `Crm\Form\ReturnPath::safe()` checks a toggle's `return_to`: it refuses `/admin`, so the dashboard sends `/admin/`.
- **Badge:** `Crm\ReadModel\TaskBadge` counts the viewer's open tasks, whatever their due date (owner 2026-11-03, was due today or overdue), the same number as the Meine tab and "Meine Aufgaben". It is a per-request memo. `OwnerOptionsQuery::all()` carries the count as a column, so a page that runs it pays nothing; any other back-office page pays one statement. `TasksNavigationItem` reads it.

**Built in #48 `crm-customer-import-2`.** No migration, no Composer or npm package. Outcome: `docs/rpi-cycles.md` `### 48.`.

- **Command:** `app:crm:import-customers <csv> [--dry-run]` (run it with `--no-debug`) reads the owner's customer sheet (`CustomerSheet`, headers verbatim, `Neue` ignored). It writes `content/owner/kunden-import-report.md` (`CustomerImport::REPORT_FILE`) on dry and real runs. The owner runs it through `bin/prod-import crm-bootstrap`, `customers-dry` and `customers` (`docs/operations.md` §Customer sheet). Row numbers are spreadsheet rows (the header is row 1).
- **Matching:** names fold in SQL with `geo_fold_de`, never in PHP. `CustomerClassifier::keyCandidates()` feeds one `CustomerMatchQuery::fold()`, and a suffixed name's key is `key(name) key(suffix)`.
  - R4, inside the sheet: rows sharing a folded name are one place when their regions fit (`SheetRegion` → canton codes; Liechtenstein is `li`, which the Liechtenstein venues carry since #46, so a Liechtenstein row can match them and still fits no Swiss canton) and they share an email or a host. Otherwise each place is named apart with ` (<Region>)`, or ` (<host>)` on a collision; failing that, it is `name repeated`.
  - R5, against the CRM: the full key, then the loose keys (before the comma, without the suffix). A hit in an incompatible canton is another place. Then host, email (companies and contacts) and `similarity(geo_search_key) >= CustomerMatchQuery::SIMILAR_NAME` (0.7) give `to check`. Every match or conflict is skipped and listed, never filled.
