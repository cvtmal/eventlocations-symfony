### Crm

Back-office companies, contacts, deals, tasks, activities and inquiries.

**Built in #27 (#27a `crm-foundation`, #27b `crm-timeline`).** Migration `Version20261031102722`; mapping `orm.mappings.Crm`; no Composer or npm package; no cron. Outcome: `docs/rpi-cycles.md` `### 27.`.

- **Tables:** `crm_companies`, `crm_contacts`, `crm_company_venues` (`venue_id` unique, cascades with the venue), `crm_activity_types` (rows, so a new type is an insert), `crm_activities`, `crm_activity_companies`, `crm_activity_contacts`, `crm_tasks`. `crm_contacts.email` is lower-case (`ContactEmail::normalise()`), NULL when empty. `crm_tasks`: `due_on` required, `chk_crm_tasks_one_object` allows at most one object. `Activity::$payload` holds an empty payload as `\stdClass`, because `[]` fails `chk_crm_activities_payload`. All times are `TIMESTAMP(0)`.
- **Routes:** server pages `admin_crm_companies*` `/admin/crm/firmen…`, `admin_crm_contacts*` `/admin/crm/kontakte…`, `admin_crm_tasks` `/admin/crm/aufgaben`, `admin_crm_tasks_toggle`, `admin_crm_company_from_venue`. JSON `admin_api_crm_{company,contact}_property`, `admin_api_crm_{companies,venues}` (lookups), `admin_api_crm_activity_{log,edit,delete}`, `admin_api_crm_{company,contact}_timeline`, `admin_api_crm_tasks`. Paths take UUIDs only, so the route walker covers them untouched. `ROLE_EDITOR` by class; `ROLE_ADMIN` for CSV, company delete, contact export and erase.
- **JSON pattern (for #28 to #30):** a thin controller, class-level `#[IsGranted]` and `#[IsCsrfTokenValid('admin_api', …)]`, payload parsed by `Crm\CrmApi` into a `Crm\Form` DTO, failures through `CrmApi::failure()`. 422 `{"errors": {"field": [...]}}`, 409 and 404 on `""`. `adminFetch` drops the 403, 404 and 409 bodies, so the island needs its own labels (`admin-ui/src/crm/failure.js`: `failureOf()`, `formFailure()`). A 2xx must have a JSON body or be a 204: a 2xx non-JSON is a lost session to `adminFetch`.
- **Inline edit:** `PATCH …/{id}` with `{"field", "value", "expected"}`. `expected` is the field's loaded value, the version (S12). `Crm\ValueObject\{CompanyProperty, ContactProperty}` allow-list the fields, `Crm\Form\PropertyConstraints` holds each field's rules for both the inline edit and the create form, `PropertyValue::canonical()` is the one canonical form. Island `record-properties`; `crm:property-saved` drives `[data-crm-follow]` and `[data-crm-refresh]` (`assets/js/admin/crm-record.js`).
- **No-op rule (P1, for every later command):** `AuditLogMiddleware` writes a row after every successful `AuditableCommand` and has no skip, so a handler returning early still leaves one. Read the current state and do not dispatch when nothing changes, as the property controllers, `TaskToggleController` and `CompanyRecordController::link()` do. The handler keeps its own check under the lock. Two simultaneous identical saves can leave one extra row, accepted. A summary never holds a value or a text, and is never empty (`changes` must be a JSON object).
- **Timeline:** `Crm\ReadModel\TimelineQuery::page()`, `Crm\TimelineProps::of()` (the island's whole props, first page included, no statement beyond `page()`), `Crm\TimelineApi`. System rows come from `AuditLogQuery::forSubjects()` with `TimelineQuery::SYSTEM_COMMANDS`, on a company with no type filter only. Both sources have UUID ids and whole-second times, so one `(time, id)` pair orders the merge and the keyset. `Crm\OwnActivity::lock()` is the write side of `TimelineEntry::canBeEditedBy()`: own, composable, not anonymised. Bodies are plain text. An entry on a contact also links its company.
- **A new entry type:** a row in your migration, a `TimelineActivityType` in your `Extension` layer, a line in `admin-ui/src/timeline/types.js`, then `activity.type.<code>` and `activity.tab.<code>` for every visible type, composable or not, and `activity.compose.<code>.{placeholder,submit}` for a composable one, added to `CrmTranslationKeysTest`'s list. `TimelineTypesPinTest` holds rows, registered types and `types.js` lines equal, and checks `activity.tab.<code>`.
- **`RecordKind` (#28):** `RecordKind::Deal` makes PHPStan flag `recordTable()`, `linkTable()`, `linkColumn()`, `taskColumn()` and the `match` in `LogActivityHandler` and `CreateTaskHandler`. Beyond that: `crm_activity_deals`, `crm_tasks.deal_id` with a replaced `chk_crm_tasks_one_object`, `Task`'s constructor, a thin `DealTimelineController`, the island's `kind` prop, and `ActivityRepository::deleteUnlinked()`.
- **Tasks:** `Crm\ReadModel\TaskListQuery` (`page()`, `openOf()`, `mine()`, `overdue()`, `isDone()`), `TaskView` (`meine`, `heute`, `ueberfaellig`, `alle`), `crm_task_due(dueOn, done = false)` → `{label, tone}`, partials `task/{_card,_toggle,_due,_object}`. Heute and Überfällig hold everyone's tasks. "Today" is `TaskDueDate::today($clock->now())`, Europe/Zurich. A company's card and the venue panel also collect the tasks on its contacts. `Crm\Form\ReturnPath::safe()` checks a toggle's `return_to`: it refuses `/admin`, so the dashboard sends `/admin/`.
- **Badge:** `Crm\ReadModel\TaskBadge` is a per-request memo. `OwnerOptionsQuery::all()` carries the count as a column, so a page that runs it pays nothing; any other back-office page pays one statement. `TasksNavigationItem` reads it.
- **Extension points and priorities:** nav Firmen 500, Kontakte 400, Aufgaben 100; widgets Meine Aufgaben 400, Überfällig 300; timeline types 400, 300, 200, 100; `VenueCrmPanel` 100; search Firmen 200, Kontakte 100. **Free: Crm nav 300 for #28, nav 200 and widget 500 for #29.**
- **Query ceilings:** the dashboard is at its 7 and the company list at its 5, both in production terms with the user refresh. #29's Neue Anfragen adds a statement to the dashboard. `CountsQueries` is the trait for a pin.
- **Bootstrap:** `app:crm:bootstrap --no-debug` and `BootstrapController` (`BATCH` 100) share `Crm\CompanyBootstrap::run()`. `CreateCompanyForVenue` takes the venue's advisory lock (`CompanyVenueRepository::lockVenue()`). A failure's reason is the constraint's name or the exception's class, never a driver message, which quotes contact values.
- **CSV:** `Crm\CsvStream::response()` holds `escape: ''` and the formula rule, so a later export goes through it.
- **Privacy:** `Crm\ContactEraser::erase()` writes with SQL, so a caller holding the `Contact` or `Company` entity must not flush it afterwards. **#29's purge through `erase()` must take the company lock before the contact, or retry (F-m7).** #29 adds inquiries to `ContactExportQuery` and extends `legal.privacy.crm`. `app:crm:purge` is #29's; `app:crm:renewal-tasks` is #28's.
- **Styles and strings:** `assets/styles/crm/_{lists,panel,properties,record,tasks,timeline}.scss`, imported in `admin.scss`, using `--a-*` tokens. Catalogue `crm`, plus `crm.*` in `validators`. Record blocks in `_record_layout.html.twig`: `record_crumb`, `record_actions`, `record_identity`, `record_quick_actions`, `record_properties`, `record_center`, `record_dialogs`, `record_aside`, `record_aside_end`.
