### Crm

Back-office companies, contacts, deals, tasks, activities and inquiries.

**Built in #27 (#27a `crm-foundation`, #27b `crm-timeline`).** Migration `Version20261031102722`; mapping `orm.mappings.Crm`; no Composer or npm package; no cron. Outcome: `docs/rpi-cycles.md` `### 27.`.

- **Tables:** `crm_companies`, `crm_contacts`, `crm_company_venues` (`venue_id` unique, cascades with the venue), `crm_activity_types` (rows, so a new type is an insert), `crm_activities`, `crm_activity_companies`, `crm_activity_contacts`, `crm_tasks`. `crm_contacts.email` is lower-case (`ContactEmail::normalise()`), NULL when empty. `crm_tasks`: `due_on` required, `chk_crm_tasks_one_object` allows at most one of company, contact and deal. `Activity::$payload` holds an empty payload as `\stdClass`, because `[]` fails `chk_crm_activities_payload`. All times are `TIMESTAMP(0)`.
- **Routes:** server pages `admin_crm_companies*` `/admin/crm/firmen…`, `admin_crm_contacts*` `/admin/crm/kontakte…`, `admin_crm_tasks` `/admin/crm/aufgaben`, `admin_crm_tasks_toggle`, `admin_crm_company_from_venue`. JSON `admin_api_crm_{company,contact}_property`, `admin_api_crm_{companies,venues}` (lookups), `admin_api_crm_activity_{log,edit,delete}`, `admin_api_crm_{company,contact}_timeline`, `admin_api_crm_tasks`. Paths take UUIDs only, so the route walker covers them untouched. `ROLE_EDITOR` by class; `ROLE_ADMIN` for CSV, company delete, contact export and erase.
- **JSON pattern (for #28 to #30):** a thin controller, class-level `#[IsGranted]` and `#[IsCsrfTokenValid('admin_api', …)]`, payload parsed by `Crm\CrmApi` into a `Crm\Form` DTO, failures through `CrmApi::failure()`. 422 `{"errors": {"field": [...]}}`, 409 and 404 on `""`. `adminFetch` drops the 403, 404 and 409 bodies, so the island needs its own labels (`admin-ui/src/crm/failure.js`: `failureOf()`, `formFailure()`). A 2xx must have a JSON body or be a 204: a 2xx non-JSON is a lost session to `adminFetch`.
- **Inline edit:** `PATCH …/{id}` with `{"field", "value", "expected"}`. `expected` is the field's loaded value, the version (S12). `Crm\ValueObject\{CompanyProperty, ContactProperty, DealProperty}` allow-list the fields, `Crm\Form\PropertyConstraints` holds each field's rules for both the inline edit and the create form, `PropertyValue::canonical()` is the one canonical form (`DealProperty::canonical()` builds on it: Rappen for an amount, `Y-m-d` for a date). Island `record-properties`; `crm:property-saved` drives `[data-crm-follow]` and `[data-crm-refresh]` (`assets/js/admin/crm-record.js`).
- **No-op rule (P1, for every later command):** `AuditLogMiddleware` writes a row after every successful `AuditableCommand` and has no skip, so a handler returning early still leaves one. Read the current state and do not dispatch when nothing changes, as the property controllers, `TaskToggleController` and `CompanyRecordController::link()` do. The handler keeps its own check under the lock. Two simultaneous identical saves can leave one extra row, accepted. A summary never holds a value or a text, and is never empty (`changes` must be a JSON object).
- **Timeline:** `Crm\ReadModel\TimelineQuery::page()`, `Crm\TimelineProps::of()` (the island's whole props, first page included, no statement beyond `page()`), `Crm\TimelineApi`. System rows come from `AuditLogQuery::forSubjects()` with `TimelineQuery::SYSTEM_COMMANDS`, on a company with no type filter only. Both sources have UUID ids and whole-second times, so one `(time, id)` pair orders the merge and the keyset. `Crm\OwnActivity::lock()` is the write side of `TimelineEntry::canBeEditedBy()`: own, composable, not anonymised. Bodies are plain text. An entry on a contact or a deal also links its company.
- **A new entry type:** a row in your migration, a `TimelineActivityType` in your `Extension` layer, a line in `admin-ui/src/timeline/types.js`, then `activity.type.<code>` and `activity.tab.<code>` for every visible type, composable or not, and `activity.compose.<code>.{placeholder,submit}` for a composable one, added to `CrmTranslationKeysTest`'s list. `TimelineTypesPinTest` holds rows, registered types and `types.js` lines equal, and checks `activity.tab.<code>`.
- **`RecordKind`:** `Company`, `Contact`, `Deal`. A new case makes PHPStan flag `recordTable()`, `linkTable()`, `linkColumn()`, `taskColumn()` and the `match` in `LogActivityHandler` and `CreateTaskHandler`. Beyond that: a link table, a `crm_tasks` column with a replaced `chk_crm_tasks_one_object`, `Task`'s constructor, a thin timeline controller, the island's `kind` prop, and `ActivityRepository::deleteUnlinked()`.
- **Tasks:** `Crm\ReadModel\TaskListQuery` (`page()`, `openOf()`, `mine()`, `overdue()`, `isDone()`), `TaskView` (`meine`, `heute`, `ueberfaellig`, `alle`), `crm_task_due(dueOn, done = false)` → `{label, tone}`, partials `task/{_card,_toggle,_due,_object}`. Heute and Überfällig hold everyone's tasks. "Today" is `TaskDueDate::today($clock->now())`, Europe/Zurich. A company's card and the venue panel also collect the tasks on its contacts and its deals. `Crm\Form\ReturnPath::safe()` checks a toggle's `return_to`: it refuses `/admin`, so the dashboard sends `/admin/`.
- **Badge:** `Crm\ReadModel\TaskBadge` is a per-request memo. `OwnerOptionsQuery::all()` carries the count as a column, so a page that runs it pays nothing; any other back-office page pays one statement. `TasksNavigationItem` reads it.
- **Extension points and priorities:** nav Firmen 500, Kontakte 400, Deals 300, Anfragen 200, Aufgaben 100; widgets Neue Anfragen 500, Meine Aufgaben 400, Überfällig 300, Verlängerungen 250; timeline types 400, 300, 200, 100, `inquiry` 75, `deal_stage_changed` 50; `VenueCrmPanel` 100; search Firmen 200, Kontakte 100, Deals 50; company tabs Übersicht 1000, Deals 500.
- **Query ceilings:** the dashboard is at its 10 (`RenewalsWidget` took the eighth; #29 took two, the inquiry badge and Neue Anfragen) and the company list at its 5, both in production terms with the user refresh. In test terms, one less: company record 7, contact record 5, deal record 6, board 5, deal list 5, company Deals tab 3, dashboard 9. `CountsQueries` is the trait for a pin.
- **Bootstrap:** `app:crm:bootstrap --no-debug` and `BootstrapController` (`BATCH` 100) share `Crm\CompanyBootstrap::run()`. `CreateCompanyForVenue` takes the venue's advisory lock (`CompanyVenueRepository::lockVenue()`). A failure's reason is the constraint's name or the exception's class, never a driver message, which quotes contact values.
- **CSV:** `Crm\CsvStream::response()` holds `escape: ''` and the formula rule, so a later export goes through it.
- **Privacy:** `Crm\ContactEraser::erase()` writes with SQL, so a caller holding the `Contact` or `Company` entity must not flush it afterwards. `app:crm:purge` goes through `EraseContact` with no lock order: a failed id is reported and the next night retries it (F-m7, N6 of #29). `ContactExportQuery` stays on CRM tables: inquiries stay out (owner's rule, #29), and `legal.privacy.crm` is unchanged. `app:crm:purge` keeps a couple with a deal (`NOT EXISTS` on `crm_deal_contacts`). `ContactExportQuery` lists a contact's deals without the amount. An erasure removes the contact's deal links; the deals stay, and a stage change never links a contact.
- **Styles and strings:** `assets/styles/crm/_{deals,lists,panel,properties,record,tasks,timeline}.scss`, imported in `admin.scss`, using `--a-*` tokens. Catalogue `crm`, plus `crm.*` in `validators`. Record blocks in `_record_layout.html.twig`: `record_crumb`, `record_actions`, `record_identity`, `record_quick_actions`, `record_properties`, `record_center`, `record_dialogs`, `record_aside`, `record_aside_end`.

**Built in #28 `crm-deals`.** Migration `Version20261031232837`; no Composer or npm package. Outcome: `docs/rpi-cycles.md` `### 28.`.

- **Tables:** `crm_deal_stages` (code-keyed rows, one `is_won`, one `is_lost`, a new stage is a migration), `crm_deals`, `crm_deal_contacts`, `crm_activity_deals`; `crm_tasks.deal_id` (CASCADE), `is_renewal`, `chk_crm_tasks_renewal`, partial `uk_crm_tasks_renewal`, which survives completion so a done renewal task is not written again. `company_id` and `owner_id` are RESTRICT, `venue_id` SET NULL, `renewed_from_id` UNIQUE. A company with a deal cannot be deleted (`CompanyHasDeals`), and `company_id` has no write path. `crm_activity_types.position` is unique: `deal_stage_changed` 5, #29's `inquiry` **6**, #30's `email_sent` **7**.
- **Routes:** `admin_crm_deals` (board; `?ansicht=liste` the list), `admin_crm_deals_{new,export,show,delete,stage,apply_tier,renew,link_contact,unlink_contact}`, `admin_crm_companies_deals`, JSON `admin_api_crm_deal_{property,stage,timeline}`. `ROLE_ADMIN` for CSV and delete. A deal is created company first (`?company=<id>`), because `VenueLookupQuery` has no company filter; `stage` is never a form field or a `DealProperty`, and a new deal starts at `new`.
- **Money:** `Crm\ValueObject\Chf` goes through strings, never a float: `parse()`, `format()`, `display()`, `plain()`, limit `MAX_RAPPEN`. `ChfTextType` is the form field, the filter `chf` shows it, `admin-ui/src/crm/money.js` does it for the island, and `admin-ui/tests/fixtures/chf.json` holds PHP and JS to one output. A `SUM` casts `::bigint`.
- **`PropertyRow` trap:** a stored amount is Rappen, so a row edits `draft` and shows `display`; `value` stays the version. A row can be `readonly`, and takes the types `date` and `amount`. A date refuses `validity.badInput`, so a half-typed date never erases a stored one; emptying all segments still saves.
- **Stage moves:** `MoveDealStage`, under the locks: the same target is a no-op, another `expected` is `StaleProperty`, lost needs a `LostReason`, going back clears `closed_at` and the reason. The activity goes on the deal and the company, never a contact. `ChangeDealLostReason` changes the reason of a lost deal alone: no timeline entry, stage and `closed_at` stay.
- **Derived stage:** `Crm\CompanyStageSync` is the one writer: `syncAll()`, `syncCompany()`, `afterDealChange()`. Only a company with a won deal is derived; it writes with SQL, so it runs after a flush, and it leaves `updated_at` alone. `ChangeCompanyPropertyHandler` refuses `stage` with `CompanyStageDerived` while a won deal exists, and `crm_company_fields()` shows it read-only.
- **Locks:** company, then deal, then venue, in every deal handler. `ApplyDealTier` dispatches `Venues\Command\ChangeVenueTier` unstamped, so both commit together. `DealTierOfferQuery` reads the offer live; no flag is stored.
- **Renewals:** `RenewalWindow` (one place for today through 60 days, Zurich), `RenewalsQuery`, `ContractPeriod`. `app:crm:renewal-tasks` reads only deals without a renewal task, so a night writes no no-op audit rows (P1). An open renewal task follows its deal: leaving won or an emptied `contract_end` completes it, a new `contract_end` moves its due date (`RenewalWindow::taskDueOn`); a done task stays as it is.
- **Cron (N7):** `app:crm:refresh-stages` and `app:crm:renewal-tasks` belong in `bin/cron-daily` after the purge lines, each with `--quiet`, because `step` prints any output and cron mails it. A count is at normal verbosity, a failure line at `VERBOSITY_QUIET`. Both lines are in `bin/cron-daily`, after `app:inquiries:purge`; they run once #20's Phase 7 enters the crontab, and until then neither runs by itself.
- **Read models:** `DealBoardQuery` (100 open, 20 newest closed per column, totals in their own statement), `DealListQuery`, `DealRecordQuery`, `DealCardQuery`, `DealStageQuery`. `DealBoardProps` and `TierOfferProps` build the island's props. `.deal-card__*` is the board's, `.deal-side__*` the side card's. `crm_deal_fields()` and `crm_deal_tone()` are the Twig functions.
- **`deal.renew.name`** gets the year as text: an integer in a `+intl-icu` message is grouped, "2'027".

**Built in #29 `crm-inquiries`.** Migration `Version20261101115604`; no Composer or npm package. Outcome: `docs/rpi-cycles.md` `### 29.`.

- **Table:** `crm_inquiry_links`, one row per ingested inquiry, key `inquiry_id`, `status` `new`, `seen`, `done` (`InquiryStatus`). `inquiry_id` cascades, so the row goes with the inquiry under `app:inquiries:purge`. `contact_id` and `activity_id` are SET NULL (N9): the row outlives an erased contact, because "no link row" means "never ingested", and the backfill must not bring a person back. Inquiry data and both mail outcomes are read live from `inquiries`, never copied.
- **Ingestion:** `IngestInquiryListener` on `InquirySubmitted` dispatches `IngestInquiry` and catches `\Throwable`, so nothing reaches the guest; it logs the class and the place, never the message. `IngestInquiryHandler` writes the contact, the entry, its links and the link row itself, and answers whether it wrote. A known e-mail is the same person: staff's values stay, only a blank first name or phone is filled. The entry's title is the venue's name, so no personal data is copied. Not auditable: the link row is its record. `InquiryActivityType` is not composable.
- **Timeline (N2, for #30):** #29 reads no `payload`; it stays `{}`. `TimelineQuery::ACTIVITY_JOINS` reads the inquiry through `LEFT JOIN`s on `crm_inquiry_links` and `inquiries`, so `TimelineEntry::$inquiryId` and `$inquiryCouple` go null with the purge or an anonymisation, and no dead link stays. #30 adds its own `payload` to `ACTIVITY_COLUMNS` and `TimelineEntry`, on top of those lines.
- **Routes:** `admin_crm_inquiries` (`?status=new|seen|done|all`, default `new`), `admin_crm_inquiries_show`, POST `admin_crm_inquiries_status`, POST `admin_crm_inquiries_resend`, all `ROLE_EDITOR`. A status change is `ChangeInquiryStatus`, audited with the status code only, read first (P1). A resend (N3) dispatches `Inquiries\Command\DeliverInquiry` on a venue mail `failed` or `pending`, not audited: it is the cure for what `app:inquiries:report-stuck` prints.
- **Commands:** `app:crm:purge [--dry-run]` erases what `CoupleRetentionQuery::expired()` returns (couples past `%inquiries.retention_months%`, no recent entry, no deal), one `EraseContact` per id. `app:crm:backfill-inquiries` ingests what has no link row and prints nothing at 0. Both print `<id>: <class>` for a failure and exit 1. In `bin/cron-daily` after `crm-renewal-tasks` and before `inquiries-stuck` (N10), so a stuck id in the same mail already has its inbox page; `crm-backfill` runs without `--quiet`, because a repaired inquiry is worth the mail.
- **F-m7 (N6):** `EraseContact` locks the contact and then writes the company, the reverse of a company delete, so the two can deadlock. No lock order is taken: each couple is one dispatch, and it takes a staff delete of that couple's company at 03:15.
- **Badge (N5):** `Crm\ReadModel\InquiryBadge` mirrors `TaskBadge`: `OwnerOptionsQuery::all()` carries the count as a second column and the inbox has it from its tabs, so only a page with neither pays its statement. No merge with `TaskBadge`, because the widget needs rows, not a count. So the dashboard added two statements: ceiling 10, 9 in test terms.
- **Priorities taken:** nav Anfragen 200, widget Neue Anfragen 500, timeline `inquiry` 75, `crm_activity_types` position 6.
- **Demo data:** `CrmStory` writes six inquiries on the linked venues with an inquiry address, each through `IngestInquiry`, with `venue_mail_status` `sent` 3, `failed` 2, `pending` 1 and links `new` 4, `seen` 1, `done` 1.
